# Reader and Orée update — 2026-11-07

The character control now keeps its larger hover size at rest. Preference-panel close
buttons have no border; language chevrons are centered SVGs. Desktop pages reserve the
same 48rem for prose with or without a right illustration; narrower screens place the
illustration below the text. Examinations sit directly against the image (0px), before any caption.
The viewer retains zoom, detail cycling and its reverse, with eye/magnifier and opposing
arrow icons, and no reset control. A reverse button is rendered only when a reverse exists.
The paper fold has been removed. Visible words now dissolve vertically over 300ms with
at most 160ms of stagger; the page itself stays opaque. Cancellation releases the word
copies and restores the original text. Reduced motion bypasses this animation.

Orée now has 38 pages per language, four learnable spells and two encounters. Its first
days include the hidden station market, meals, correspondence with the ordinary world,
practice, a safe paper-dragon exercise and the broken guardian. Choices affect bonds,
recognition of the missing word and the closing vocation. Exactly one illustration is

local source library. The built publication contains 23 referenced media files (5,274,904 bytes).
Both languages now explain the invitation, practical spell instructions, gateway safeguards
and the accident concretely. The warning, failed barrier and teachers' response establish
why a beginner has to hold the gateway briefly rather than repair it alone.

Game results retain optional actual dice faces alongside the existing roll/RNG snapshot.
Displaying a die consumes no randomness. A check is staged in `game.pendingCheck` without
advancing Ink or RNG. `rollCheck` commits its result, and `finishCheck` advances its branch
only after confirmation. Reloading before or after a roll preserves that state. Combat
actions are selected first, then executed by the explicit Roll dice button. Dice settle
over 850ms before another action or continuation is available. Hero HP appear only in the
character sheet; committed attacks pulse the board and damage shakes it. Learning a new
spell pulses its event border. These effects respect reduced motion.
The optional state fields preserve schema compatibility; a save from before the story
rewrite still requires an explicit restart because its Ink digest differs. Character stat bonuses
are derived in RulesEngine and supplied by StoryDirector; React displays the result.

and modal controls live in `src/shared`; speech settings/references, native effects and
