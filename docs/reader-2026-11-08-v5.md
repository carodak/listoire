# Reader and Orée 1.2

This replaces the interaction details in the earlier November 8 reader report.

## Reading and transitions

Progressive display types graphemes at a smooth animation-frame cadence, capped at
the following word of narration. It no longer reveals whole words at each audio
progress update. Pausing stops the cursor; a delayed frame cannot skip a large run
of text. Device-voice boundaries are tested with a controlled browser speech clock.

Page departure dissolves the title as well as the visible prose. Its duration is
500 ms longer than the previous implementation (920 ms plus at most 220 ms of
letter staggering). Illustrations and the heading rule fade during departure.
Progression waits for all animations; the new scene, including its heading, fades
in over 1.4 seconds. Reduced-motion preferences skip these effects. The toolbar
remains outside the animated board.

Reader scenes are centered. Image elements have no paper background. The story
uses centered illustrations between paragraphs with no lateral text wrapping,
plus right-column portraits for tall characters. The preferences close button
aligns with the first title line. The character toolbar uses a person icon.

## Choices, saves and combat

Check choices describe the attempted action and show the actual attribute, die
size and threshold. Selecting the action commits its roll immediately. The result
dialog offers Continue after the visual dice settle, with a green/red localized
verdict and the expression `roll ≤ threshold?`. Previous page restores the check's
pre-roll character/RNG state when that origin exists. Legacy unrolled checks still
have a safe retry path; completed legacy rolls retain their saved outcome.

The xorshift generator and rejection sampling were retained. Deterministic long-run
tests verify every d6/d20 face within 1.5% of its expected count across respectively
600,000 and 2,000,000 rolls. Restoring the same pre-roll checkpoint intentionally
reproduces the same result. Visual dice never consume gameplay randomness.

`GameState.pendingEvent` retains an already committed item/event or completed combat
on the current Ink frame. Its notification appears before departure. Dismissing it
finishes the story choice and starts the transition. Restoration resumes the pending
notification; storage failure retains both its mutation and choice for retry.
Event choices and action references are validated against the frozen frame.

Combat shows two framed square portraits above the combatant labels. Opponent damage
and hero STR/DEX/WIL use a compact row above their respective HP bars. There is no
second character sheet or XP display in the combat modal. Opponents without a
registered portrait use original vector emblems for the paper creature/stone figure.
No unrelated source-library illustration is substituted.

The four actions form a 2×2 grid: basic attack, ability, item, flee. Information
controls explain success versus damage dice, thresholds, probabilities, equipment
and secondary effects. Numbers come from RulesEngine through StoryDirector.
Abilities list only known compatible abilities. Available healing consumables can
be used once: healing is capped at max HP, consumes the item and takes a turn with
normal retaliation/effect expiry. Orée supplies one 3-HP balm before training.

Spell effects have a stronger temporary light wash and an arc across the viewport
below the toolbar. Effects return to the selected paper color and honor reduced motion.

## Story and existing editor copies

Orée now contains 41 bilingual pages. Three ordinary-world pages introduce the
workshop, its owner, a clock delivery and the canal before the invitation. Two
layered opening scenes use relevant existing canal/city art. Other pages remain
unillustrated where the library has no suitable picture. Source libraries are unchanged.

Vague metaphors were replaced with observable actions and dialogue, including the
apple's fall and the guardian's approach. The final short narration paragraph was
rewritten with context; text hashes prevent reuse of the former short clip. Exact
listening review of newly generated speech is still required: text/hash checks do
not prove that a generative voice never adds a sound or word.

The new release uses checkpoint namespace `oree-v1-2`; former `oree` saves remain
stored and are not interpreted against the changed Ink program. The expanded story
starts a new playthrough. Recognized older default editor projects are archived in
full before installation of 1.2, so normal reading does not silently keep loading
old prose. Renamed custom projects, offline drafts and newer concurrent saves are
preserved. History contains the complete former project for restoration/export.
