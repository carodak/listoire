# Reader adjustments, November 8 — v6

This supersedes the combat layout, text pacing and instant-reveal behavior in v5.

- Combatants share five grid rows: square portrait, role, name, stats and HP.
  A missing character name stays blank. Wrapped opponent names reserve the same
  row height for both sides, so their HP bars align.
- The training opponent uses Ysée's actual sketch portrait: she directs the paper
  dragon exercise. Its label names both Ysée and the dragon. The guardian uses the
  hooded-traveler gallery sketch. Inline vector stand-ins were removed. Registered
  custom portraits are retained, and source-library files are unchanged.
- The combat modal fills the available viewport below the toolbar. Only its stage
  can scroll; the four primary actions remain outside that scroll region. Portraits,
  heading and result dice are smaller. The existing 112-pixel die renderer is scaled
  visually without changing the committed roll or consuming more RNG.
- Information panels use short instructions and compact tables. Basic attacks show
  each d6 face and its damage with the character's current attribute/equipment.
  Ability tables show ranks 1–4, success thresholds, probabilities and successful
  damage, highlighting the current rank. The rules still give the same damage on
  a successful ability use at every rank; rank improves its success chance. These
  tables are computed in RulesEngine and tested against actual combat outcomes.
  Information panels stay within the viewport even inside a scrolling ability list.
- Narrated text advances through the next word as the spoken word progresses,
  rather than snapping its target to whole-word ends. A bounded adaptive letter
  clock spreads coarse speech updates over time. Unicode graphemes, pause/resume,
  monotonic reveal and the one-following-word limit remain intact.
- Text reveal and media skipping are separate. `PresentationState.skipMedia` is set
  by restore, reduced motion or explicit cinematic skipping; instant text and
  `revealRemaining()` do not complete/stop image effects. A fresh instant-text scene
  starts its image animation normally. Fullscreen blocks still complete in order;
  only mounted, reached paragraphs announce their completed text, so later cues
  cannot run early behind a cinematic.
- Spell arcs run for 2.6 seconds (1.5 seconds longer). The learned-spell border runs
  for 2.9 seconds; story spell flashes last 2.7 seconds. Other board beats are exactly
  two seconds: the watches stopping, the levitating coin, crossing the gateway,
  waking the training dragon, the greenhouse impact and the return of light.
  Combat impact shake lasts two seconds and targets the stage, keeping action
  buttons stable. Effects remain cancelable and honor reduced motion.
- Existing recognized Orée 1.2 editor copies receive missing gallery portraits and
  the selected direction cues, archiving the complete former draft first. Scripts,
  voices, release and checkpoint namespace stay unchanged. Both compiled Ink hashes
  are unchanged (`99beeab44707…` EN and `3284cb15812c…` FR), so current 1.2 saves
  remain compatible. Custom portraits/names and unrelated authored cues are preserved.
  Missing custom encounters and replaced text segments do not trigger needless
  archive writes or introduce cues referring to nonexistent segments.
