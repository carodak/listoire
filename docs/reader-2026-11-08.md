# Reader corrections — 2026-11-08

This supersedes the combat, dice, text-lead and illustration behavior in the November 7 report.

- Page/scene overlays cover the viewport below the reader toolbar. The toolbar stays outside
  board shakes and departure visibility changes. React Bits page layers also sit outside the
  shaken board. Combat feedback and spell-learning borders use the same viewport bounds;
  full-screen cinematics leave the toolbar visible.
- Departure animates individual graphemes, attached to the document before their animations
  start. Progression awaits every animation. A browser test pauses the outgoing animations
  and verifies that the next passage cannot appear until they resume and finish.
- Text reveal leads narration by just the following word, across paragraph boundaries and
  without splitting Unicode graphemes.
- Images decode before display. Smart cropping withholds the original image until the final
  crop is decoded; camera effects wait for their image planes. Isolated, untransformed static
  objects use their natural proportions rather than a letterboxed camera canvas. Examinable
  images have no lower mask/editorial fade. Known story images carry dimensions to reserve
  their initial aspect ratio. Source-library files are unchanged.
- The d6 and d20 use the supplied `6Silver` and `20Silver` meshes and matching diffuse textures
  from `assets/dnd-dice-set/source/DiceSet.fbx`. `tools/extract-dice.py` extracts only these two
  models, validates their UV slots, and produces optimized application assets. OGL was already
  installed; no new dependency was added. Source hash, user authorization and the unverified
  provider-license status are recorded separately in the dice-assets provenance file.
- Every die face retains its original texture. Landing orientations map to the committed
  result; rendering consumes no gameplay randomness. UI continuation waits for the visible
  roll. Missing graphics/texture support retains a numerical result and cannot block the
  adventure indefinitely. Texture loading has a five-second limit.
- In combat, Attack, Flee and a selected ability execute their roll directly. Abilities are
  grouped in a separate menu containing only known offensive abilities permitted by the
  current rules. Success probabilities come from RulesEngine through StoryDirector and are
  shown in button tooltips; ability options also show them as text. A normal attack always
  deals damage under the current rules; its 100% is a hit probability, not a victory forecast.
- Hero HP sit beside opponent HP. The combat character sheet retains its portrait/attributes
  and hides its HP and XP. Entering or restoring a page never opens combat without an explicit
  Fight click. An already staged/restored encounter offers Fight again in the passage.
- Check choices/modal headings state the action and actual attribute/die type. Checkpoints
  now retain the character, RNG, XP, base HP and preceding result from before a check.
  Previous page restores that state, rather than restoring a completed roll. Back to the
  passage also cancels an open new check. Storage failures preserve the committed checkpoint.
- Legacy unrolled checks acquire an origin when rolled. Legacy completed checks without an
  origin keep their immutable result: the lost pre-roll state cannot be reconstructed safely.
  Their back control is disabled with an explanation; continuing does not award XP twice.
